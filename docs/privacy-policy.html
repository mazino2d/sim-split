SimSplit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Short version: To share groups with friends, SimSplit asks you to sign in with Google and keeps your groups in Google's Firebase. Only the members of a group can see it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you can delete your account at any time from the app.

This policy covers the SimSplit web app at simsplit.web.app and SimSplit 2.0 and later for Android and iOS. Earlier versions of the Android app (1.x) work entirely offline: they collect nothing and store everything only on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Your account. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's name, email address and a unique account ID through Firebase Authentication. We do not receive your Google password.

What you put in the app. The groups you create or join, and everything in them:

  • Group names and settings (such as the currency)
  • Member names you or other members enter, and which member is you
  • Expenses (description, amount, payer, date, category, note and how it is split)
  • Settlements between members
  • The group's activity history: who added, edited or deleted what, when, and the values before and after

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to keep your groups in sync between your devices and the other members of each group, and to show who made each change. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

3. Who Can See Your Information

  • Members of a group can see everything in that group, including the activity history and the names of the members. Server-side security rules enforce this: someone who is not a member cannot read or change the group.
  • Anyone with a group's invite link can join that group. Its owner can reset the link at any time so that old links stop working.
  • Google stores and processes the data on our behalf as the provider of Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Hosting (Firebase privacy and security).

SimSplit contains no advertising networks, analytics or tracking SDKs, and no crash reporting services.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your groups are stored in Cloud Firestore in Google Cloud's Singapore region (asia-southeast1). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. A copy of your groups is also kept on your device (in the app's private storage, or in your browser for the web app), so the app keeps working offline; changes made offline sync once you are back online.

On Android, SimSplit opts out of Android backup: the copy on your device is not included in Google cloud backups or device-to-device transfers.

5. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Sign out removes your groups from the device. They stay in your account.
  • Leave group removes your access to a group. Your member name and the expenses recorded under it stay in the group for the other members.
  • Delete account (Settings → Delete account, in the app or on the web) deletes your account and removes your data from this device. You leave every group: groups where you are the only member are deleted with everything in them, and in groups with other members your member name and its expenses stay, so their balances remain correct.

We keep your data until you delete it or your account.

6. Device Permissions

SimSplit uses the internet to sign in and sync. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or other device resources.

7. Children's Privacy

SimSplit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
